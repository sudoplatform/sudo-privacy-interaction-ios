DataHolderScanSummary

public struct DataHolderScanSummary : Equatable, Sendable

Email interaction metrics captured during a single scan for a specific data holder.

A scan summary aggregates the emails observed from a data holder within a single scan’s date range, along with read and marketing engagement rates and a per-category breakdown.
